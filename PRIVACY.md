# Privacy Policy — Technician Radio Study

**Your study stays on your phone.**

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy describes the current iPhone version of Technician Radio Study, developed by Gray Mongoose.

## Data collected by the app

The app works offline and does not require an account. Gray Mongoose does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the app. The app contains no advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or active account synchronization.

## Information stored on your device

Your language and appearance preferences, learning progress, saved flash cards, answers, and mock-exam state are stored locally in the app’s storage. You can remove local app data by uninstalling the app. Any device backup or restoration is controlled by your Apple settings and Apple’s services.

## Support messages

If you contact [ham@graymongoose.us](mailto:ham@graymongoose.us), your email address and the information you choose to send will be used to respond and investigate your request. Please send only information needed to explain the issue. You can use the same email address to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

## TestFlight beta feedback

If you choose to send beta feedback through TestFlight, Apple may provide your submitted feedback, screenshots, and diagnostic information to the developer under Apple’s TestFlight terms and privacy practices. This information is separate from the app’s local study data.

## External links

Links to NCVEC, ARRL, or other external websites open services that have their own privacy practices. Gray Mongoose does not control those services.

## Policy updates

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change and revise the effective date above.
